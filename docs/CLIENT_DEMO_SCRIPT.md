3. Open Studio Pro: pick sample jersey front/back, colors, team name and number, upload PNG logo, move object, save and export mockup.
4. Show Artwork Intelligence: upload a simple high-contrast logo, inspect image data; conversion to SVG should be shown only after successful live testing. For complicated posters/photos explain why tracing may fail or be unsuitable.
5. Show Customer Orders, Staff Marcus and Admin screens, with synthetic demo data only and authorized test accounts.
6. Explain provider/cost panel, Groq primary and Kimi backup, Alibaba image generation pending identity/document verification.
7. Future workflow: ERPNext sales and manufacturing, NAS/Drive retrieval, payment integration, shipping, notifications.

Demo disclaimers: sample garment NOT print-ready, vector trace is NOT recovery of exact original layers, current orders are demo records, and AI image creation is NOT live.
